Adds authentic EU5-flavoured descriptions to all kinds of miscellaneous entries: countries, dynasties, cultures, culture groups, religion groups, religions, religious schools, religious aspects, gods, chivalric orders, holy sites, languages and language families! Hover over one to read it.

[b]7,057[/b] descriptions in total. Countries include those that only appear later in the game: released or restored states, polities that rise after 1337, and formable nations such as Spain and Great Britain. Dynasty coverage is for dynasties with living members at that start. The dynasty tooltip displays the description through its head.

Every description was written with the best AI models (Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Terra and others), drawing on sources in English and local languages, mainly Wikipedia, through several rounds of drafting, editing and spot-checking. I used the best models available, at a large cost in tokens and money, but for this mod, I think it was worth it :)

All my future descriptions will be added to this mod; there will be no new separate mods. So the character, tile and this mod are best subscribed together.

If you find a mistake, please leave a comment. I'll keep maintaining and updating this mod!
