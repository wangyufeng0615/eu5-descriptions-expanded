Adds EU5-flavoured English and Chinese descriptions to [b]7,075[/b] countries, dynasties, cultures, religions, gods, chivalric orders, holy sites, languages and more. Hover over one to read it. Where the game already has a description, it is kept and continued.

⭐ [b]Strongly recommended: subscribe to the other two EU5 mods as well.[/b] Together the three cover the map, its people and its countries.
