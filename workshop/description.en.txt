[h1]Descriptions for countries, cultures, dynasties and more[/h1]

Vanilla EU5 describes only 22 of these.

This mod adds descriptions to [b]5,294[/b] of them: every country with land in 1337, every culture, every dynasty alive in 1337, and every language, language family and holy site. Hover over one to read it at the bottom of the tooltip.

[list]
[*]Supports English and Chinese
[*]Keeps EU5's flavour
[*]Can be added to an existing save
[*]Ironman and achievement compatible
[*]Works together with my character and tile description mods
[/list]

Every description is based on sources, mainly Wikipedia in English and local languages, and went through several rounds of checking. I used the best models available and went through a huge number of tokens. But for this mod, I think it was worth it :)

All my future descriptions will go into this mod. Religions, culture groups and more will be added as updates. If you find a mistake, please leave a comment. I'll keep maintaining and updating this mod!
