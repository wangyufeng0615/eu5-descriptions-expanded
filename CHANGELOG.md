# Changelog

## 1.2.3 — 2026-11-22

- Editorial quality pass across 66 high-frequency countries, cultures, dynasties, holy sites, and religions in English and Simplified Chinese: unified cross-mod historical terminology, improved prose flow, and aligned EN-ZH details.

## 1.2.2 — 2026-11-15

- Rewrote the descriptions of the remaining countries in the same style as the major ones, bringing out what each place was known for.

## 1.2.1 — 2026-11-14

- Rewrote the descriptions of about 200 major countries to bring out what made each one distinctive, with the people, battles and scenes behind their rise and fall.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-14

- Rewrote most country descriptions: about 40% shorter, with more on what happened between 1337 and 1837.

## 1.1.4 — 2026-11-14

- Removed the English copies for other game languages added in 1.1.3; the download is back to its earlier size.

## 1.1.3 — 2026-11-14

- Other game languages (French, German, Spanish and more) now show the English descriptions.

## 1.1.2 — 2026-11-14

- Compatible with Glorp UI: the country tooltip keeps Glorp's army breakdown and also shows the description. Place this mod below Glorp UI in your playset.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-11-14

- Coverage reaches 100% (7,075 descriptions): add the last 18 entries, including Pirates, Mercenaries, the old German kingdom and the Tristutik Gaccha religious school.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-14

- Add 953 descriptions (7,057 in total): 865 countries that appear later in the game, including formable nations, plus religion groups, religious schools and chivalric orders.
- Long tooltip descriptions now scroll fully; the language tooltip no longer pushes its description off screen.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-13

- Add 6,104 English and Simplified Chinese descriptions across ten categories.
