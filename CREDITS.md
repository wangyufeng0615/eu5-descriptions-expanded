# Sources and credits

`sources/sources.jsonl` (in the mod) and the `sources` field of `data/entries.jsonl` (in this repository) list, for each entry, the pages consulted when writing the description. Most are Wikipedia articles in English or other languages; Wikipedia contributors are credited through those pages and their history. The list records research provenance; it does not mean Wikipedia or any other source endorses this mod or that every statement was independently verified.

The descriptions condense, reorganise and translate consulted material with AI assistance, and the source pages are not reproduced. New description text is offered under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (see `LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`): keep these credits and the source links, mark your changes and keep the share-alike terms. Wikimedia's [Terms of Use, section 7](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use#7._Licensing_of_Content) describe reuse of Wikipedia text.

`in_game/gui/zz_lde_history_tooltips.gui` contains the vanilla EU5 country, culture and dynasty tooltip definitions with a description block added; they are Paradox's files, shipped so the block can be shown, and are not relicensed. The `replace/` localization covers 832 existing EU5 description keys. Each entry retains the original Paradox text and appends the new paragraphs. The CC BY-SA 4.0 licence applies only to the new paragraphs.
